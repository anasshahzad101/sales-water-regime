6.6

Accounts

Answers: which companies are we chasing, and what is their status?

Behaviour
  • Search matches company, brand and plant. Filters: sector, city, priority, owner, plus Unassigned only and Dormant only toggles.
  • Scope defaults to My accounts for Sales and All for Managers. Dormant rows show the days since the last visit in amber.
  • 25 rows per page on desktop. Phones use “Load 25 more”.

Add company

Only the name is required. Possible duplicates show while you type.

Behaviour
  • Duplicate check (debounced, server-side): fuzzy match on name, brand and group. Shows up to 3 matches with “Open existing”. It doesn’t block saving, because a new plant of the same group is valid.
  • Sub-sector stays disabled until you pick a sector; its options come from the shared list.

6.8

Account detail

Header with facts and actions, contacts with one-tap Call / WhatsApp / Email, deals and the visit timeline.

Behaviour
  • Claim account: one click, no approval. It shows the current owner in a tooltip, then a toast with Undo. Recorded in the audit log.
  • Contact actions use tel:, https://wa.me/ and mailto:, each 44px tall on phones. Delete always asks for confirmation; Undo appears in the toast afterwards.
  • CEO: no Log, Claim, New deal or contact editing. A Read-only pill sits in the header. Calling and emailing still work.
