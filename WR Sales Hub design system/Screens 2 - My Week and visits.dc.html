Screens 2 · My Week, Log visit, Visits

The daily loop for Sales: what needs me now, record what happened, look back. Every artboard is live, so you can click, type and set outcomes in place.

6.3

My Week

Home for Sales, Manager and Admin. Answers: what do I need to do today?

Behaviour
  • Desktop quick outcome: one click saves, then a toast offers Undo for 6 s. Skipped, or any outcome on an overdue visit, opens step 2 inline.
  • Phone: Set outcome opens a bottom sheet with the list; a details step appears only when a reason is needed.
  • Countdown: recalculated every minute. Amber under 12 hrs, red Overdue after 48. The server re-checks the rule when saving.
  • Due this week: ticking an item moves it to Done, with Undo. The My Week badge counts visits awaiting an outcome and turns red if any are overdue.

6.4

Log a visit

Answers: record what happened. One screen with sensible defaults. Only Company and Outcome are required.

Behaviour
  • Date and time default to now (PKT). “Now” resets it. Outcome defaults to Pending, which starts the 48-hour countdown.
  • CompanyPicker: type to filter by company, brand, plant or city. Arrow keys and Enter work. The list shows priority and owner so you pick the right plant.
  • DealPicker only lists open deals for the chosen company, plus “No deal”.
  • Skipped shows the skip-reason chips (required). Saving goes back to where you came from (account page or My Week) and shows a toast with Undo.

6.5

Visit history

All my visits. Managers see the whole team and get a Salesperson filter and column.

Behaviour
  • Active filters show as removable chips with “Clear all”. On phones, filters live in a bottom sheet with a live result count.
  • Export to Excel downloads the current filtered list (.xlsx). The button shows a spinner while the file is built.
