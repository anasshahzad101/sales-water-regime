Clickable prototype
  1. Sign in → My Week → set an overdue visit's outcome. The late reason step appears, and the My Week badge goes from red to green.
  2. Log a visit from an account. The company is pre-filled, and after saving you land back on the account with the new visit on the timeline.
  3. Move a deal to Lost. A reason from the list is required, Other needs a note, probability goes to 0%, and you can Undo.
  4. The CEO opens the Cockpit and drills into a flag. Everything stays read-only, all the way down to the deal page.
The top bar switches device, theme and role. Changes you make carry across pages until you restart.
Direction (approved)
  • The deep-teal sidebar is the one brand block that's always visible. Content sits on calm mist (light) or night (dark).
  • Bright green is only for “Log a visit”. Primary buttons use green-700 (5.6:1); in dark mode, green buttons have dark text (6:1).
  • Statuses use 7 colour pairs, and every pill has an icon and a label. Small text uses #637469, because muted-2 is only 3.1:1.
  • Phones get a 5-tab bar with a raised Log button. The CEO gets the Cockpit only, with a read-only indicator everywhere.
