Team Pulse board, new deal, and the deal page with stage changes. Every artboard is live, so you can click, type and set outcomes in place.

6.9

Team Pulse

Answers: where is every deal right now? Six stage columns, each with a count and total value.

Behaviour
  • Cards show the days in stage; over 30 days they turn red with “Stuck”. A passed close date gets its own red line.
  • No drag-and-drop: stages change on the deal page, so a Lost reason can never be skipped. Columns scroll sideways under 1600px.
  • Phone: horizontal stage tabs with counts, then that stage’s cards and total.

6.11

Deal detail

Header KPIs, a stage control, proposals with status updates, related visits and change history.

Behaviour
  • Change stage: the next stages apply at once, with Undo. Won asks for confirmation and sets 100%. Lost opens the reason picker (from the shared list). An optional note becomes required for “Other”. Lost sets 0%.
  • Proposal status is changed from its pill (Submitted / Revised / Accepted / Rejected), and each change is logged. Add proposal is an inline form; the next reference number is pre-filled.
  • The full value is shown on hover; the KPI strip always shows the full amount under the short one.
