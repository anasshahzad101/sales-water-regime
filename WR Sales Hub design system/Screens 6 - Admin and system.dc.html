Screens 6 · Admin, shell & system

Users, audit log, navigation states, toasts and error pages. Every artboard is live, so you can click, type and set outcomes in place.

6.13

Admin · Users

Add, change role, reset password, activate or deactivate.

Behaviour
  • Reset password creates a temporary one and forces a change at the next sign-in. Copy it, or regenerate it. Deactivating takes effect at once (with Undo) and ends their sessions.
  • An admin can’t deactivate themselves: the item is disabled and says why.

5

App shell & feedback

Navigation by role, user menu, phone More sheet and the four toast tones.

Behaviour
  • Phone tabs: My Week · Accounts · Log (raised) · Pulse · More. More holds Visits, Cockpit, the admin pages, theme and sign out. The CEO has no tab bar, only Cockpit.
  • Toasts sit top-right on desktop and above the tab bar on phones. Success toasts disappear after 6 s; errors stay until dismissed.
