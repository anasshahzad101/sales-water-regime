Screens

A calm, dense work tool with one loud button.

Tokens and components for WR Sales Hub. Every colour has a light and a dark value, and every status has an icon and a label as well as a colour. Use the toggle at the top right to switch this page between light and dark. The status section always shows both.

Calm and denseMist surfaces, 14px body text, tight rows. Built for all-day use.
Urgent items firstOverdue items, countdowns and flags go at the top of every list.
Never colour aloneEvery pill has an icon and a label as well as a colour.
One loud buttonBright green is reserved for "Log a visit".

Countdown rulesFrom the visit time until 48 hrs later: whole hours ("31 hrs left"), and minutes under 1 hr. Amber under 12 hrs. Red “Overdue · N hrs” after that, and the late reason becomes required. The bar shows how much of the 48 hrs is left.
Flag thresholdsStuck: an open deal more than 30 days in the same stage. Dormant: an owned account with no visit in 60 days. Close passed: an open deal past its expected close date. Outcome overdue: no outcome 48 hrs after the visit.
